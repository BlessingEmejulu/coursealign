Can you explain the difference between a Process and a Thread in operating systems?

AI

Of course!

  • Process: An executing program. It has its own isolated memory space. It is considered a "heavyweight" unit of execution.
  • Thread: A smaller unit of execution within a process. Multiple threads can exist within the same process and share the same memory space and resources. This makes them "lightweight".

Think of a process as a house, and threads as the people living inside sharing the kitchen and living room!
